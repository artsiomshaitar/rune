- **Rune runs on Apple TV.** `rune` builds for `aarch64-apple-tvos` and `aarch64-apple-tvos-sim`, and `rune::tvos::TvApp::new(spec).run()` runs an `AppSpec` as a whole tvOS app: a UIKit scene with a `CAMetalLayer`, driven by a `CADisplayLink` that pauses when idle. The Siri Remote reaches the app as keys through `rune::remote` (arrows, Select as Space, Menu as Escape, held arrows repeat, touch-surface swipes step), also available to any embedder as `LayerHost::remote`; `TvApp::menu_exits` hands Menu to the system on the first screen. `scripts/tvos-app <package>` bundles, signs and launches an app on the tvOS simulator, and `examples/tvos-hello` is the smallest one. winit 0.30.13 is vendored under `vendor/winit` with tvOS treated as iOS, and `rune`'s new build script puts an empty `libdispatch.a` on tvOS's link path for the `dispatch` crate winit pulls in.
