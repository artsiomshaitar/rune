- **A tvOS app can play video under its own UI.** `rune::tvos::set_underlay` puts a native `CALayer` (an `AVPlayerLayer`) under the app's layer, which draws transparent while it's there (`LayerHost::set_transparent` for any embedding host); `El::system_material` then draws a `UIVisualEffectView` (or `UIGlassEffect` on tvOS 26) under the element, following its frame, corners, opacity and soft clips, so the system blurs the video under the controls. The Siri Remote's Play/Pause now reaches the app as `Key::PlayPause` instead of the system, so an app that relied on the system's handling sees the key instead.
