- **Spatial focus moves focus with the arrows by geometry, as the tvOS focus engine does.** `Cx::spatial_focus` turns it on, and a Siri Remote turns it on by itself: an arrow nothing else took moves focus to the nearest focusable element that way (in the half-plane past the focused element's centre line; overlap across the move first, then the nearer, the better centred, document order), and with nothing focused focuses the last focused element or the first. `El::focus_section` makes a row or a nav bar one target from outside that lands on its last focus, else the member nearest the press. `El::on_focus_move` replaces a move from the focused element outward (`FocusMove` in, `FocusTarget::To(key)`, `Held` or `Default` out), and `El::on_focus_held` hears a press with nowhere to go. An app's `on_key` that takes an arrow still bypasses it; Tab is unchanged.
