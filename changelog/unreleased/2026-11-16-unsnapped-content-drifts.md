- **Text, shapes and icons move with a box that doesn't pixel-snap, and land without a jump when a scale settles.** An element with `pixel_snap(false)` that an `El::offset` or `live_offset` moves by fractions of a pixel now carries its content with it: it draws where it rests, on whole pixels, moved by the offset (text in quarter-pixel glide phases), instead of each piece stepping a whole pixel while the box's fill and edges glide. Every glyph takes the same drift, including a first glyph whose pen rests on a pixel boundary. Text in a box whose scale moves (a transition's pop, a press's dip) lays out from where it will rest, so the frame the scale lands moves none of its glyphs; a label at a fraction of a point used to shift each glyph by up to a pixel there. At no offset and at rest everything draws exactly as before.
